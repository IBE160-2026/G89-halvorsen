# Material rights: Norwegian study-aid tests and demonstrations

Research date / accessed: **2026-11-02**. English evidence digest. No project files were read; no actual course PDFs were selected. Conclusions below use sources retrieved in this run. This is a bounded risk-management recommendation, not legal clearance for a particular file, institution, AI service, or examination.

## Recommended decision

Use a small **own-authored fixture containing no third-party text, diagrams, photographs, or slide extracts** for local tests, external AI requests, examiner demonstrations, screenshots, saved generated answers, and public-repository examples. Document the author's permission for these uses. Alternatively use verified CC0 or CC BY material and keep a provenance/attribution record. This is a project recommendation inferred from MR1–MR8; it does not assert that every other use is unlawful.

Do not treat legitimate course access, a download button, personal study, an educational purpose, or a no-training provider setting as a blanket grant for all later uses. Keep **access, copying, processing by an external provider, examiner sharing, and public publication** as separate decisions (MR1–MR4). Copyright clearance also does not settle personal-data handling (MR5–MR7).

### MR1 — statutory baseline and private copying

- **claim:** Åndsverkloven §2 assigns copyright to the creator of an original literary/artistic work. Section 3 reserves permanent/temporary reproduction and making available to the public, including altered forms and adaptations. Section 26 permits limited private copies of a disclosed work subject to conditions, including non-commercial purpose and no unlawful source; those copies cannot be used for another purpose. This provision alone does not establish permission for the proposed external-service workflow or public repository.
- **URL:** [Lovdata, Åndsverkloven](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2018-06-15-40/KAPITTEL_6-2); [same Act, §26 indexed text](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2018-06-15-40/KAPITTEL_9)
- **publisher:** Lovdata; legislation of Norway, responsible ministry Kultur- og likestillingsdepartementet.
- **pub_date:** Act 2018-06-15; retrieved indexed consolidation reports last amendment 2024-12-13, effective 2025-01-01; EEA-footnote correction 2025-11-28.
- **accessed:** 2026-11-02
- **confidence:** High for reproduced statutory wording; medium for current-consolidation completeness because direct opens returned HTTP 405 and evidence came from publisher-indexed search text.
- **class:** Statute / bounded legal inference.

### MR2 — quotation has a purpose-limited test

- **claim:** Supreme Court decision HR-2022-1113-A, paragraphs 43–46, reproduces §29: quotation from a disclosed work must accord with good practice and be limited to what the purpose requires. This is a contextual test, not a general PDF/excerpt quota or an automatic demonstration exception.
- **URL:** [Norwegian Supreme Court, HR-2022-1113-A](https://www.domstol.no/globalassets/upload/hret/avgjorelser/2022/juni-2022/hr-2022-1113-a.pdf)

- **pub_date:** 2022-06-02
- **accessed:** 2026-11-02
- **confidence:** High for quoted statute; medium for any application to an unseen screenshot or excerpt.
- **class:** Primary court decision / bounded inference; not an AI-upload ruling.

### MR3 — university agreement is scoped

- **claim:** Kopinor/UHR's model agreement covers institutional internal use, 2025–2029. Applicable direct/open/subscription licences matter. Sections 4–5 define permitted recipients and closed-network sharing, prohibit internet publication under this agreement, restrict systematic storage/indexing, and exclude use as machine-learning/generative-AI training data absent separate agreement or law. Section 2 does not authorize adaptation/translation. These terms do not themselves establish that an individual student's external AI app is covered. Training exclusion alone does not resolve inference-time upload.
- **URL:** [Kopinor, university/college model agreement 2025–2029](https://kopinor.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Monsteravtale-universiteter-og-hogskoler-2025-2029.pdf)

- **pub_date:** Negotiated June 2024; retrieved publication path February 2026; operative period 2025-01-01 to 2029-12-31.
- **accessed:** 2026-11-02
- **confidence:** High for model text; institutional adoption, amendments, and applicability unverified.
- **class:** Primary agreement / scope limitation.

### MR7 — CC0 alternative

- **claim:** CC0 waives copyright/related rights to the legally permitted extent and permits copying, modification, distribution and performance, including commercially, without permission. It does not affect other people's privacy/publicity rights or patent/trademark rights; CC has not verified a labelled work's copyright status. Record provenance even though attribution is not a stated CC0 copyright condition.
- **URL:** [Creative Commons, CC0 1.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- **publisher:** Creative Commons
- **pub_date:** Version 1.0; page publication date unknown.
- **accessed:** 2026-11-02
- **confidence:** High for deed summary; medium for provenance recommendation.
- **class:** Primary public-domain dedication summary / operational inference.

### MR8 — AI outputs require their own review

- **claim:** The government's practical workplace AI guide recommends using own/cleared input, checking provider conditions, and not assuming outputs are free to reuse, especially commercially or publicly. It identifies reproduction/adaptation questions for input and resemblance-to-source issues for output. This supports reviewing saved summaries, answers, and screenshot content, not only the original PDF.
- **URL:** [Regjeringen, practical AI-assistant guide, copyright section](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/dokumenter/ki-assistenter-i-arbeidslivet-en-praktisk-guide/id3109040/?ch=10)

- **class:** Primary government practical guidance; not legislation or clearance.

## Operational handling derived from the evidence

| Use | Proposed project control | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local private tests | Keep any uncleared course content outside tracked fixtures and shared artifacts; local processing does not create publication permission. | MR1, MR3 |
| External commercial AI | Use cleared fixture input. A no-training option addresses a different question from reproduction/transfer authority. Record provider and permitted processing. | MR1, MR3–MR4, MR8; inference |

| Public Git repository | Include only cleared PDF/text, excerpts, saved answers and screenshots, with attribution. Review the visible content of images and generated artifacts. | MR1–MR3, MR5–MR8; inference |

Suggested fixture manifest fields: title, author/rightsholder, source URL, version/access date, licence and licence URL, scope/excluded elements, changes, local/external-AI/demo/public-sharing permission, and attribution string. For explicit permission, name the AI provider/process, allowed retention/training, intended audience, redistribution and derivative outputs. These are evidence-preserving project recommendations (MR3–MR8), not statutory form requirements.

Practical CC BY attribution pattern: **“[Title] — [Author], [source link], CC BY 4.0 [licence link]. Changes: [description].”** Preserve supplied notices; do not imply endorsement (MR5–MR6).

## Remaining uncertainty and boundaries

The institution, actual content rights, applicable signed Kopinor/subscription agreement, AI provider terms, and examination rules were not examined. No source establishes an entire-course exception authorizing unrestricted AI transfer. An author's permission may need to exclude material they do not control (MR6). Whether a specific excerpt or generated response reproduces protected expression needs case-specific assessment (MR1–MR2, MR8). This digest does not conclude that all generated summaries infringe or that all commercial AI uploads are categorically forbidden.

Prefer the cleared-fixture route now. If authentic course files become essential, obtain institution/rightsholder clarification against the exact planned processing and sharing, rather than expanding this research into a claim of definitive legal clearance (recommendation based on MR1–MR8).
