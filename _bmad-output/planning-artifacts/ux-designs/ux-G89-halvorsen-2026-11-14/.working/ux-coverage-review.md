## Round 21: assess all 17 cases, reuse four outcome presentations

All 17 cases are relevant v1 requirements or test fixtures. They do not require 17 distinct screens or custom solutions. Reuse **document error**, **generation error**, **usable-content notice** and **demo information**, alongside the existing operation-specific **pending** presentation. A document error can offer another PDF through confirmed New session; extraction uncertainty can also offer manual Try again. A generation error retains valid study material and offers an applicable manual retry or setup explanation. A usable notice accompanies available content; demo information explains saved playback and unsupported live actions.

Of the 17 fixtures, **13 are blocking errors, three are usable-content notices and one is demo information**. Blocking applies to the rejected document or attempted output, not to valid content elsewhere in the active session. The [internal scenario mapping](reliability-scenarios.md) retains reason IDs, publication/state rules and detection/test prerequisites. These IDs and technical causes belong in development/review evidence, not new student controls or student error messages.

Student feedback says what happened, whether usable material remains and what the student can do next. Technical distinctions such as malformed fields or an unlocatable excerpt remain internal. Plain copy must still be honest: a generic creation failure must not claim all content is factually verified, and a reduced-set notice must not present invalid items as usable.

Cards/Quiz failure and pending states preserve the valid Summary, any other existing learning content and its progress. Retrying one function does not clear another function's material or practice. The fixtures represent retained state; they do not establish production cancellation, late-result isolation or all completed-attempt combinations. At mobile app widths up to 540px, Flashcards/Quiz content boxes, notices and actions use shared centred alignment, as do loading/error status boxes. Summary reading prose/notices, the source reader and desktop layout retain their existing treatment. These are refinements of the selected UI system, without advanced PDF detection or new v1 features.
